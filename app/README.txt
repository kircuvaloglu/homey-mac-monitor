Your Mac mini does a lot of quiet work in the background, and now your home can keep an eye on it. See at a glance how hot it runs, how busy it is and how much disk space is left, and get a nudge when something needs attention, like a full disk or a missed backup.

Let your Mac take part in your Flows as well: dim the lights when you walk away from it, keep it awake while a download finishes, put it to sleep at night and wake it again in the morning. A small helper runs on each Mac, and everything stays on your own network.
